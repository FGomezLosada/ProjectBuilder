description=All-in-one QGIS project builder: packages, reprojects and clips your layers and web services into a ready-to-work, self-contained project
about=Starting a GIS project usually means a morning of copying, reprojecting and clipping layers one by one. ProjectBuilder does it all at once: pick in a single panel the layers (local or network folders, the open QGIS project, PostGIS, SpatiaLite or GeoPackage connections), the web services (WMS, WMTS, WFS: your favourites, your QGIS connections or a built-in, automatically checked catalogue of Spanish public services: IGN, Cadastre, IGME, regional SDIs), the work area and the CRS, and it builds a self-contained .qgz project: vector layers in a single GeoPackage (or one per layer, or their original format), rasters as compressed GeoTIFF, reprojected to the project CRS and clipped to the work area (polygon layer, selected features or rectangle, with a buffer), with their styles and SVG icons, web services and print layouts centred on the area. Includes drag and drop, layer reports before and after (PDF, HTML, CSV) and saved configurations to repeat the same kind of project with one click. Runs in the background with no external dependencies. User interface in Spanish.
